## What I did / reviewed
- Rules from Requirements Specification v2.0: F-36/F-37 (more than 9 h / 3 h), F-38, F-39, F-40, the F-42
  example (records at 00:00 and 20:00 → one period 09:00–20:00), F-46 (stored timestamps) and NF-08. The
  30 % threshold comes from client answer CL-11 (`docs/decisions/client-clarifications-2026-11.md`).
- Inactivity is derived from the absence of records (Scope §3.2), so the alarm is computed from the stored
  timestamps, with no writes. It opens at "previous record + threshold" and resolves by itself at the next
  record. A SMAC record is a record in any of its three tables (TD-07).
- The response fits the frontend model of the Inactive devices tab (`inactiveDevices.types.ts`, SCRUM-235):
  device, node, zone, substation, circuit, threshold and periods `{start, end|null}`.
- The user scope is applied in SQL, like the other endpoints (F-10).
- Ran: 510 unit tests, lint, build; e2e on PostgreSQL with Colbits' triggers (95 + 3, 7 new); a duplication
  check (0 clones). With generator data, the real API listed the 3 silent devices and the inactivity scenarios
  in F-40 order, and closed the alarm of a device that reported again at that record.

## Second commit: low-energy alerts (F-44, F-45)
- `GET /device/low-energy` (`low-energy-alerts.service.ts`, DTO `dto/low-energy-alerts.dto.ts`): one active alert per
  device of the scope whose latest stored `supercaps_per` (Falcon) or `battery_per` (SMAC) is below its NF-08
  threshold. A reading at or above the threshold clears it (F-44/F-45 acceptance). `since` = first reading of the
  current low streak.
- Kept in the device module, not in `GET /faults`, because the fault list and its frontend do not handle the
  LOW_ENERGY type yet. It can be merged there later with the team.
- Ran: 515 unit tests, lint, build; e2e 98 + 3 (3 new: lowest first, exactly 30 % is not low, a reading above clears
  it, start of the streak); duplication check 0 clones.
